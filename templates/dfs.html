Bring-back — opposing offense in the QB's game: 38/89 (42.7%), off by default because the research calls it optional. The 6x pill counts projected players at ≥6 pts/$1k; in the study 76/89 winners had one, but the research is explicit that this is a retrospective outcome, not a projection target — so it's reported, never forced. Ownership rules are deliberately absent: the study says the ownership sum and low-owned counts must not become mandatory lineup rules, and we don't have pre-lock projected ownership here. This is a construction framework, not a tested profitable strategy.
