CALIBRATION

STATED PROBABILITY vs REAL OUTCOMES

{% endblock %} {% block content %}

Every probability ScoreEdge shows is an estimate. This page checks the estimates against reality — no fudging. Each time a hunt is served we log exactly the players and percentages we printed; once those games go final we join them to the real scoreboard and mark each pick won or lost.

A perfectly calibrated 40% shot should come in about 40% of the time. The tables below let you see where we're hot (predictions land above the stated rate) and where we're cold. Small samples swing hard — we label sample sizes honestly and never invent results.

Method & limits

Predictions are logged verbatim as served and de-duplicated by day. Outcomes are joined from the NHL and MLB public scoreboards (goal scorers and home-run hitters on final-game boxscores), matched by team and player last name.

Brier score and log-loss are computed over resolved binary picks only; pushes and still-pending picks are excluded. The market-edge section settles logged moneyline/spread/total spots from real final scores.

These are honest, self-reported numbers from our own logs — not betting advice or a promise of future results.
