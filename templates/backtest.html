IF YOU BET EVERY SUGGESTION · FLAT 1U · REAL OUTCOMES

{% endblock %} {% block content %}

Every value spot a board posts is journaled and, once the game finishes, settled against the real final score. This page replays that record as if you had bet each one for one flat unit, and reports what actually happened — no estimates, no look-ahead.

The replay universe is the edge journal unioned with settled odds-bearing picks from the suggestion ledger (deduped, the same set the learning model trains on). Hunts have no market odds and DFS has no standard payout, so both get hit-rate only and stay out of the units math.

Method

Universe = settled edge-journal records (value spots resolved to real finals) ∪ settled odds-bearing picks (picks.to_journal_like), deduped. Stake 1u per bet; win pays decimal-1, loss pays -1, push 0. ROI = units / (wins + losses); pushes return stake and are excluded from the denominator. Splits are by market, by the edge bucket at post time, and by the book whose best price was taken. Stats-based replay · NOT a market forecast · past results do not guarantee future returns.
