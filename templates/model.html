This is the final feature: a tiny, regularized logistic model that learns from the games we've already settled in the edge journal. It ingests nothing synthetic — every labeled example is a quote that we later resolved to a real win/loss.

We compare the model's probability to the consensus-implied probability (fair market read from the slate) and report OOS Brier score on a held-out split. Below you'll see the current weights, evaluation, and a forward read on the latest cached slate (unsettled, so no look-ahead).

Method

Features: log-odds of consensus-implied win prob, edge points (best vs consensus), home-side flag, market dummies (spread/total). Regularized logistic regression, L2=1.0, 300 iterations, random 70/30 split with fixed seed; OOS metrics only. Labels are the union of journal.settled_records (real final scores) and settled odds-bearing picks from the suggestion ledger (picks.settled_picks → to_journal_like), deduped. Predictions on latest slate use cached/slate data; unsettled rows have no look-ahead.

This is the last planned feature per your instructions (learning model reserved for the end). It learns from past settled outcomes to improve future accuracy. Stats-based estimate · NOT a market price.
