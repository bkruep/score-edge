Method

Features: log-odds of consensus-implied win prob, edge points (best vs consensus), home-side flag, market dummies (spread/total). Regularized logistic regression, L2=1.0, 300 iterations, random 70/30 split with fixed seed; OOS metrics only. Labels from journal.settled_records (real final scores). Predictions on latest slate use cached/slate data; unsettled rows have no look-ahead.
