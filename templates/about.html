ABOUT SCOREEDGE

EDGE-FIRST BETTING & FANTASY ANALYTICS

{% endblock %} {% block content %}

ScoreEdge turns the raw sportsbook market into a clean read. For every game and player prop we pull prices from the major US books, remove the vig with a Shin de-vig, and rank the spots where one book's price genuinely beats the consensus — the "edge" in points of implied probability, not a noisy ratio that explodes on longshots.

The dashboard, per-sport boards, parlay builder, Best Bets Today, DFS optimizer and the player hunts (Touchdown, Goal and Home Run) all run on the same idea: show what the market is actually saying, label every estimate honestly, and never invent a number we don't have.

What ScoreEdge is not. It is not betting advice, a guarantee, or a tout service. Probabilities are estimates derived from third-party data; edges are a difference of opinion, not a promise. Bet only what you can afford to lose, and only where it is legal.

Where the data comes from

  • Odds & lines — real sportsbook prices (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football/basketball, MLS), de-vigged in-house.
  • NFL player stats — nflverse / Sleeper feeds for the stat-driven boards.
  • Golf — DataGolf finish-position model and top-20 market prices (bring your own API key).
  • Horse racing — FormFav race form and (on higher tiers) bookmaker prices and model win probabilities (bring your own API key).
  • NHL / MLB hunts — the leagues' public stats APIs, combined with park and weather context.
