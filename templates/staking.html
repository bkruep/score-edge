Every board posts a best price that beats the consensus line. That gap is the edge, so the consensus price is our probability estimate and the best price is what we can take. Kelly sizes a bet as edge / (decimal−1); we scale it down (default quarter Kelly) and cap it, because one edge estimate is noisy and a slate's spots rise and fall together.

Sizing does not create edge — it only decides how much of a real edge to risk. A bigger stake on a fake edge just loses faster.

Method

Consensus implied probability p = 1/decimal(consensus); full Kelly f = (p·d − 1)/(d − 1) with d the decimal price taken; the stake is f × fraction, capped at the max-stake setting, applied to the bankroll. The replay compounds Kelly stakes off the running bankroll and compares to a flat 1u stake on the same settled bets. Sizing math only · no bet is certain · past results do not guarantee future returns.
